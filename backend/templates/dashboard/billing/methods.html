Eligibility first. Stripe does not onboard businesses in Pakistan directly (it is not on Stripe's supported-country list). PayPal does not currently offer normal receiving accounts in Pakistan. Payoneer offers payment requests for Pakistani businesses. For most Pakistan-based agencies the dependable options are local bank / IBFT / Raast and international wire (SWIFT), plus a Payoneer request link. Never use another person's account or a false address to get around provider rules. Online gateways stay switched off until you confirm your provider account is approved.
