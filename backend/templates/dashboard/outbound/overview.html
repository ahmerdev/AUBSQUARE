How it works

  1. Find – search OpenStreetMap by business type and city, or import a CSV. Only real businesses are added.
  2. Audit – we read each website (safely) and score the gaps you can sell: no WhatsApp, no booking, weak SEO, no HTTPS…
  3. Approve – a short message is drafted from the real findings. Nothing is sent until a person approves it.
  4. Convert – replies stop the sequence. Interested prospects become Leads, then Proposals.
